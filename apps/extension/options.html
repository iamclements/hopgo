Hopgo settings

The domain where your Hopgo redirect Worker is deployed. Short links are built from this.

One-click domain setup

Deploy the Hopgo redirect Worker to one of your Cloudflare domains. Sign in via the popup first, then load your domains and deploy. This sets your short-link domain automatically.
.
Links are served from this subdomain (e.g. go.example.com). Leave it blank to use the apex, but only if nothing else is served there. A proxied DNS record is created for it.
