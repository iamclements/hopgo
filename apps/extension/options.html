hopgo Settings
Before you start
  • You need a free Cloudflare account (no credit card required).
  • No domain? Use the free workers.dev path below - no setup needed.
  • Have a domain on Cloudflare? Use the one-click domain setup to serve links from your own domain.
Free setup - no domain required
Deploy to your free Cloudflare workers.dev subdomain. No custom domain, no DNS setup. Sign in via the popup first.
Your links will be served from hopgo.<your-subdomain>.workers.dev. Every Cloudflare account includes this for free.
Custom domain setup
Deploy the Hopgo redirect Worker to a Cloudflare domain you own. Sign in via the popup first, then load your domains and deploy.
.
Short links will be served from this subdomain, e.g. go.yourdomain.com. A proxied DNS record is created automatically.
